## The Exchange Workflow
Money exchange over the counter.  
* Transaction Initiation: The Customer starts the process by requesting a cash exchange.  
* Transaction Execution: The agent looks up current exchange rates, applies necessary fees, and processes the payment.  
* Validation: Every transaction includes a "Validate Exchange" step to ensure the trade is legitimate.

## Documentation
* Identity Verification: The agent must verify the customer's details before finalizing the trade.  
* Evidence of Transaction: The Agent generates a physical or digital receipt that includes a timestamp and a clear breakdown of the paid and received amounts.
